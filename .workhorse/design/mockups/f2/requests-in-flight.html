Proposed legend entries

held / sent / recv T in the idle column: a request in flight, received but not yet sent, sent with no reply begun, or streaming back. T counts from arrival. +N: N more in flight.

~model, dim with its ceiling: the model the session was routed to, none served yet.

✗: failed. The newest request to finish ended in failure. Red like the other failures, and the glyph carries it without colour.

The idle column widens to fit the longest stage cell; the name column gives up the room, as it does for every other column.
