The third entry is a credential that resolved to no account, and it is the selected row, so the footer's naming control is live. It carries no number, since a number is assigned when an account becomes known, so it sits under a dim ? — unknown, which the legend already defines. Its requests are counted rather than hidden: they are held against the digest and move to the account when it is named.

from the upstream against you named it carries the provenance in words rather than in a mark, so nothing new has to enter the legend and the distinction survives NO_COLOR. Nothing on the page adds or removes an account: an account the operator could create is one no credential names, which nothing could ever be served on.
