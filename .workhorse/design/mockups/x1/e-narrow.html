20 · the ? overlay on a route page (120x40)

The operator pressed ?. Keys first, then marks: every glyph the page draws that the dashboard legend does not already carry has a row here. The path glyphs, the dim row, 1 → 2 and the page strip are new; ‹N and … N more already exist on the dashboard for accounts and are reused for pages and rows, so their legend wording widens. V and a are gone: there is nothing to validate or activate. m is the one consent left, and it arms.
