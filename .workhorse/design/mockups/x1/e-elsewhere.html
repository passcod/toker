Direction E: where everything that is not a route lives

Routes are the pages. Everything a route page refers to is a table of its own, one tab each: 2 rules (what sends a request to a route), 3 models (what a block means), 4 history (what was live). Providers and settings stay on the config page, and each record says which routes use it. Tab 1 is the route overview and the route pages, drawn in e-routes.html.
