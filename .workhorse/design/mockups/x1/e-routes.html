Direction E: one page per route, read top to bottom — the route pages

Each route is a page a request travels down: matched by (the rules that send a request here), handled by (the blocks that check or change it), sent to (the candidates, in fallback order). The rail glyphs are the path; reading downward is reading in the direction the request goes. Data is the live store; where the captures do not show a value (a fallback chain, a classifier id, an upgrade policy field) it is plausible and marked in the report.
