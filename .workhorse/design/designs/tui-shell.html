Every style in the TUI appears above: the header chips reversed, the four marks, the three bar kinds, the meter bands, the absence vocabulary, and dim for idle and for a subordinate clock.

Palette reference

green    a reading that is fine, or better than asked for
yellow   worth noticing, or a release that spends
red      exhausted, failed, or about to be
cyan     identity rather than judgement
blue     the page-switch chip, and nothing else
gray     chrome that is not content
plain    everything else, which is most of the screen

bold     emphasis, since colour is not
dim      absent, inactive or subordinate
reversed a handle to press, or a row in focus
[b] burn an armed control

Plain white text is the most prominent thing on the screen at 14:1, while red sits at 3.77:1 and yellow at 8.69:1. Colour here is a de-emphasis channel: a meter at 96% reads softer than one at 85%. Reaching for a saturated red produces a warning panel where the real screen is a calm readout.
